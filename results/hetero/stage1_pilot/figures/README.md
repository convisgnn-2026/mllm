# Stage 1 motivation figures

Generated by `bench/hetero/plot_stage1_motivation.py` from stage1_summary.json and stage1_cells.csv. Seed 901, 600 s observation; exploratory results, no confidence intervals.

- fig_stage1_latency.pdf: double-column-width draft. Successful-request p90 only; failed requests are excluded from latency quantiles. Offered load is LLM 2α requests/s and DiT 0.3α requests/s. Representative four configurations, not all seven. P/D: prefill 4090, decode 3090. DiT split: denoising 4090, encoder/VAE 3090.
- fig_stage1_slo_choices.pdf: all seven configurations, three illustrative SLO profiles from the declared grid. Each number is min(LLM joint TTFT/mean-decode-TPOT attainment, DiT E2E attainment); not a pooled request fraction. Errors/timeouts/unfinished are retained in denominators. Asterisk means both services reach the 90% target. Profiles (TTFT seconds, TPOT milliseconds, image E2E seconds): A=(0.5,20,10), B=(1,12,3), C=(1,30,5). Profiles are selected after exploration to illustrate tradeoffs, not independent validation.

Suggested caption: SLO-dependent placement choices on an RTX 4090–RTX 3090 testbed. Cells show the lower of the two service-specific attainment rates; * denotes that both services meet the 90% target. At low offered load, the preferred feasible configurations depend on the latency requirements. At the higher tested load, only the P/D-based configurations meet any of these three profiles. Each P/D engine has 4 GiB KV cache (8 GiB total), versus 4 GiB for single-engine configurations.

PDF/SVG are vector exports; PNG is a 300-dpi preview. IEEE-compatible sizing and typography are used, but final placement should follow the target venue template. No claim of statistical significance or DiT-split superiority is implied.
